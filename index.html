On a phone drag one finger to move around · two fingers: pinch to zoom, twist to turn, move both up or down to tilt · Block, Curve, Ring: tap the ground and drag its handles (its middle moves it; arrows for walls, floors, a ring's size, a curve's ends and bend); Done (or Cancel). Tap a built block for its arrows and the blue tabs on its roof: drag one inward to step back. Tap a selected roof to build on it, or the floor of a hole in it · Extrude: tap a panel, drag the arrows round it to stretch the choice, Next, then drag the arrow · Road: tap, tap, tap, Done; tap a road to drag its ends · Trees and Land paint with one finger

Camera right-drag to turn · middle-drag or Shift+right-drag to slide · wheel to zoom · WASD move · QE turn

Build drag along the first wall, then swing the mouse out sideways to make the block wider; release, move up for floors and click. Start on a roof to build on top.

Push and pull drag any wall: only that wall moves, out or in; drag an end wall to lengthen or shorten. With Floors (4), drag a roof up or down.

Extrude (9) drag across a wall to choose panels, then drag from them. In Build out mode dragging out builds a box onto the wall (a bay, a wing, a bridge); in Dig mode dragging in digs them out (a loggia; right through: a hole, or an arch at the bottom). Holes may overlap. Straight blocks only.

Step back grab a roof edge and drag inward to stack a smaller copy on top: an end edge trims that end, a long edge moves that wall in, a corner steps in all round (a pyramid). On a straight block it then stays editable: drag its walls, or drag its middle to slide it (it snaps to centred and flush); click beside it to go on to floors.

Snapping G grid on/off · Shift+G turns the grid to the block under the cursor. New blocks also line up with the ones around them (pink guides). Hold Alt for free angles and lengths.

Walk (V) then click where to stand: a street, a roof, a ledge, the floor of a hole. Drag to look around (up and down too), click to walk there (another roof or the ground: go there), WASD to walk (Shift faster), wheel for the lens, P or the shutter for a picture, Esc to leave
